No monthly fees, no hidden costs. SoundScript uses your own ElevenLabs API key – you pay only for the minutes of audio you generate. Perfect for individuals and small teams who want professional results without recurring bills.

Zero cloud storage. All your scripts, voice settings, and audio files remain 100% local. SoundScript does not upload, store, or share your content – only your direct API calls to ElevenLabs. Your privacy is non‑negotiable.
