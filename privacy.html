Sensitive Information. We do not process sensitive information.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

Information automatically collected

 In Short: We do not collect IP addresses, browser information, cookies, analytics, usage logs, or crash reports. The only information generated automatically by the app is a hashed machine identifier used for license enforcement.

SoundScript generates a cryptographically hashed machine identifier on your device to support license activation and anti-piracy enforcement. This identifier is derived from the device's network adapter address and operating system name, then hashed (SHA-256) so it cannot be reversed to identify you or your physical device. This hashed identifier is transmitted to Gumroad's verification API only during license activation.

We do not collect IP addresses, browser type, operating system details beyond what is used to generate the hash, device model, location, or any usage analytics.

Google API. Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

13. LOCAL-FIRST ARCHITECTURE

 SoundScript is a desktop application designed with a local-first privacy model. Your scripts, API keys, license file, cached audio, and downloaded AI models are stored only on your own device. BitProgram operates no servers that collect, store, or process your personal information.

The only data that leaves your device are: (1) text you submit to cloud AI services (ElevenLabs, Google Gemini) when you select a cloud engine, (2) your license key and a cryptographically hashed machine identifier sent to Gumroad for license verification, and (3) model files downloaded from public repositories on first use. All other operations — including all local AI generation, caching, and file management — occur entirely on your device.

You can delete all data associated with SoundScript at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing its data folder. Because no personal information is stored on BitProgram servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.
